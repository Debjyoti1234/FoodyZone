OUR STORY

Food brings us together.

FoodyZone is a student-built online food ordering system created to demonstrate practical web development concepts using HTML, CSS and JavaScript.

Project objectives

  • Explore restaurants and menus.
  • Manage a cart and apply discount coupons.
  • Calculate delivery fees, platform fees, tax and payable amount.
  • Save addresses, favourites and demo orders locally.
  • Demonstrate responsive design and interactive JavaScript.

Technology stack

HTML5 · CSS3 · Vanilla JavaScript · localStorage · Lucide icons

This is an academic demonstration. It does not process real payments or provide real-time delivery services.
